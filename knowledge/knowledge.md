## What I'm working on right now

I work as a Student Research Assistant (HiWi) in Data Science at the
Institute for School Development Research (IFS) at TU Dortmund, about 7
hours a week. I'm part of the team working on the IGLU/PIRLS project — an
international study on reading literacy in schools. So while some data
scientists optimize ad clicks, I'm helping figure out how kids around the
world are doing at reading. Slightly more wholesome, arguably less lucrative.
